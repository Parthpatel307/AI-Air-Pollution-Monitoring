# 🌍 AirGuard AI

### Environmental Intelligence for Air Pollution

> **Observe. Understand. Predict. Act.**

AirGuard AI turns air-quality data into actionable environmental intelligence by combining **machine learning, Gemini AI, multimodal vision, and real-time monitoring** in one platform.

It is built for two audiences:

**🏙️ Citizens** — understand air quality, pollution risks, and environmental conditions.

**🛡️ Authorities** — investigate pollution, review evidence, monitor incidents, and make informed decisions.

---

## ✨ What makes it different?

**🔮 Predict**
Machine-learning models forecast near-term AQI from pollution and environmental patterns.

**🧭 Attribute**
Probable pollution contributors are estimated from pollutant and weather patterns.

**🧠 Explain**
Gemini turns environmental measurements and model outputs into understandable insights.

**📸 Verify**
Vision AI analyzes submitted evidence while explicitly representing uncertainty.

**⚡ Respond**
Live environmental intelligence connects monitoring, alerts, evidence, and authority workflows.
